About Me

I'm Nicholas, a data professional with a background in research, analytics, and applied AI. I enjoy asking the right questions, working through messy datasets, and making complex findings understandable.

My projects range from customer behavior and healthcare data to experimental research on language processing. I work across the analytical process — cleaning data, testing ideas, building models, and presenting actionable insights.

What motivates me most is connecting rigorous analysis with clear communication, so the work is useful beyond the code.
